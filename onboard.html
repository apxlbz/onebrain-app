Before we start

Welcome to OneBrain.

You’re the first person from here, so the next few minutes set up the memory everyone on your domain will share. It takes about five minutes, and nothing here is hard to change later.

What OneBrain does

Captures
Email, meeting notes, calendars and boards flow in on their own, read-only, once a source is connected.
Distils
Each item becomes short facts — decisions, commitments, who knows what — deduplicated and kept current.
Answers
Your team searches it in the dashboard; your AI tools call it directly. Every answer shows where it came from.

What setup covers

  1. Name your organization. A display name for the memory. 30 seconds
  2. Connect sources. Pick what should be remembered, or none for now. Optional
  3. Choose a plan. Your trial credit is already in; nothing is charged until billing activates. 1 minute
  4. Verify it works. Live checks against every connection, so you leave knowing it runs. Automatic
Only one person on your domain needs to do this.
