OneBrain

Your organization's shared memory.

OneBrain listens to the places where your team already works — email, meetings, calendars, boards — and distills them into one searchable memory of decisions, commitments, people, and context that the whole organization shares.

What OneBrain does

Companies forget. People leave, threads scroll away, decisions get re-litigated. OneBrain keeps the institutional memory current, so anyone can ask what was decided, who committed to what, and why.

Connects your sources

One-click, read-only connections to Gmail, Google Meet notes, Google Calendar, and Trello. Each member connects only their own accounts.

Extracts what matters

Raw messages and transcripts become concise facts — decisions, tasks, commitments, relationships — deduplicated and kept up to date.

Answers your team

Search, browse people and topics, explore the knowledge graph, or plug the memory straight into your AI tools over MCP.

How OneBrain uses your Google data

Access is read-only, per member, and revocable at any time from your Google account or from OneBrain's Sources page.

  • Gmail (read-only) — reads recent work mail (promotions and social are excluded) to capture decisions and commitments made over email. Message content is processed into short factual notes; mailboxes are never shared between members.
  • Google Drive (read-only) — reads Google Meet notes and transcripts stored in your Drive so meetings become part of the shared memory.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) — reads events, attendees, and dates to anchor the memory in time — who met whom, and when.

OneBrain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Private by construction

Every organization's memory is isolated by email domain. Members sign in with their work Google account; everyone on the domain shares one memory, and nobody outside it can reach a single row. Data is stored encrypted at rest, and source tokens live in a managed secrets vault.
