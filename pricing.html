Pricing

Plans are sized around facts and agents, not tokens or credits. Free holds 500 facts and 2 agents; Pro and Team lift those limits with no change to how OneBrain works — the same recall, the same provenance, the same data controls.

What you’re billed on

No credits, no token math, nothing to meter. Two things decide your plan:

  • Memories. Every fact OneBrain remembers for you counts once. Free keeps up to 500; Pro and Team are unlimited.
  • Agents. Each tool or harness that calls the API — Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, a script — is one connected agent. Free allows 2; Pro and Team are unlimited.

Calling recall and remember is unlimited and already included on every plan — API calls themselves are never metered or billed, so you can predict your bill before you write a single line of integration code.

A fair-use cap can pause a runaway writer. It is not a charge. recall keeps answering from what is already stored.
