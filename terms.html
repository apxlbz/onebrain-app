The service

OneBrain provides a private memory shared across the AI agents you connect. You explicitly remember facts; agents recall them. A fresh sign-in is a personal namespace. Shared spaces are opt-in for people who already belong to one. Automatic ingestion is off: OneBrain does not read Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or Trello on its own. Plans are sized by stored facts and connected agents. Recall and remember are included on every plan.

Your account and namespace

You sign in with Google. A fresh sign-in gets a private personal namespace. People who already belong to a shared space can select it. A shared email domain does not add you to one.
